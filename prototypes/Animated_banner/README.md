# Tennis AI Coach — Blurred HTML5 tennis video demo

Open `design/pages/index-video-test.html` through a local server from inside `design/`, e.g. `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open `http://localhost:8000/pages/index-video-test.html`.

The HTML includes a **real HTML5 `<video>`**, blurred video playing at 0.3× speed, and pause-synchronized coaching messages. Each loop plays the first 4.5 seconds of source footage at 0.3× speed, with a 5.5-second freeze at source timestamp 2.900s and a second 3.5-second freeze at 3.700s. The first pause starts after about 9.7 seconds of viewing; the second follows 2.7 seconds of playback after the first resumes. The resulting loop takes about 24 seconds plus seek latency. Shorter clips loop at their natural end and skip freeze timestamps beyond their duration; the source file is unchanged. The fluid page wrapper grows to 1360px, with the desktop video enlarged by a further 12% (about 989px wide and 665px tall). It extends left beneath the text section with a broader fade; the text stays above the video. An upper-right microphone has a visible 1.6-second glow pulse and expanding ring during playback and coaching pauses. At 1100px and below, the hero stacks into one column; the microphone and small message bubble remain inside the responsive video area. No external player or tracking library. Original `index.html` remains untouched.

Video is served remotely from Wikimedia Commons for this test (internet connection required). For offline use download the clip from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2018_Davis_Cup_Americas_Zone_-_Uruguay_vs_Mexico_-_04.webm and save it as `design/assets/tennis-rally.webm`.

**Freeze-frame lines:** Manually positioned green strokes follow the supplied 1280×720 reference images: six strokes at 2.900s (arms and legs), then eight at 3.700s (arms, torso and legs). These are illustrative annotations, not detected joints or live tracking; no MediaPipe/model is loaded. After each exact-frame seek settles, the strokes draw one after another (300ms per stroke, starting every 350ms). All strokes are visible after about 2.05s at the first pause and 2.75s at the second. They remain until that pause's total duration (5.5s / 3.5s) ends, then disappear before playback resumes at 0.3×. Each pause happens once per loop, with its own coordinates; timers suspend when the banner/tab is hidden. Reduced-motion playback does not auto-start. `video-demo.js` owns the coordinates and pause timing; the transparent canvas maps source pixels through the existing centered cover crop and scale(1.07), including responsive resize and pixel density. Replacing the video requires recalibrating these manual coordinates. The cues are scripted visual feedback for this prototype, not inferred measurements or generated advice. No microphone permission, speech synthesis or audio playback is added; the video stays muted.

To use with your up-to-date Codex-modified pages, copy only `pages/index-video-test.html` and `video-demo.js` into your `docs/prototypes/design/` directory; these use your existing `styles.css` and `app.js`.


**Coaching interaction:** The three upper connection/analysis/demo badges are retained; only the motion-tracking metrics panel and bottom coaching pill are removed. At the 2.900s / 5.5s hold, four messages appear individually: “Good arm position”, “Bend your knees”, “Toss the ball higher”, and “Keep the racket position stable”. At the 3.700s / 3.5s hold, the sequence is “Keep the racket position stable” then “Follow through”. Each message rises into view with a 400ms fade and slight scale, drifts upward, then fades upward for the next cue. Compact screens reserve space below the upper badges to avoid overlap. Message selection and animation progress use the same elapsed hold time as the green lines, with no separate animation timers. Resume, seek and leaving the banner clear messages; hidden-tab/offscreen time does not advance the sequence. Reduced motion disables the microphone pulse and message movement. Existing hero text, video source, crop, speed, freeze durations and line rendering remain unchanged.
